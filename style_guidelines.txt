style_guidelines.txt

Couleur
        Background color : Grey (#f5f5f5) and white
        Text color : Black (#222), blue (#0066cc) and white
        Primary blue : #0066cc (icon.svg, links, main buttons)
        Dark blue (hover) : #004c99
        Borders : #ddd

Mise en page
        Forms in a centered white card, max width 400px
        Rounded corners (8px), light shadow around the card
        Light grey page background
        Top bar with a Home button

Icons :
        icon.svg : blue square (#0066cc) with a white "u"

Typographie :
        "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif (sans-serif)
        Title centered, 1.8rem on sign up / sign in

links : black and blue underline
        Links in blue #0066cc, underlined

Buttons style : rectangulaire avec forme arrondie (6px)
        Filled button : blue background (#0066cc), white text, dark blue (#004c99) on hover
        Outline button : blue border, white background, light blue (#e6f0fa) on hover
        Round slider buttons (40px), white with grey border
