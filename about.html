About uMentorship

Connecting students with the guidance and experience they need to succeed.

Why uMentorship?

Navigating university and planning a career can be challenging. Students often need advice, direction, and support from people who have already experienced similar challenges.

uMentorship helps students connect with mentors who can share their knowledge, experiences, and advice.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make mentorship more accessible and help students build the skills, confidence, and connections they need to achieve their goals.
