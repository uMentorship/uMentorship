How it works

  1. Create an account and tell us which courses you're taking.
  2. Browse mentors near you who have passed those courses.
  3. Send a request to the mentor you like and pick a time to meet.
  4. Meet before your exam, in person or online.

About uMentorship

uMentorship connects students who need help preparing for exams with mentors in their area. Our mentors are students and graduates who have already passed the courses you're taking, so they know what the exams look like and where people tend to get stuck.
